# Sakhi Clinic Beta 1.0 - Risk Register

## Status
- Version: 1.0
- Purpose: Capture the major implementation risks for the Beta 1.0 release slice
- Scope: Consultation-first release

| Risk | Description | Probability | Impact | Severity | Mitigation | Detection | Recovery | Owner | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Voice interruption | Recording may stop unexpectedly or become ambiguous during live consultation. | Medium | High | Critical | Use a defined voice state machine and explicit pause/resume/retry flow. | Monitor recording-state events and error logs. | Allow the doctor to re-enter recording without losing context. | Senior Mobile Architect | Recording state is always visible and recoverable. |
| Transcript loss | A transcript may not be routed correctly or may be lost between capture and save. | Medium | High | Critical | Route transcript through a single save path and persist draft state. | Validate transcript creation and save events. | Restore from draft or allow manual re-entry. | Principal Software Architect | Transcript appears in the correct consultation field or is clearly recoverable. |
| Permission denial | Microphone permission may be denied or revoked. | Medium | High | High | Show a clear prompt and fallback path. | Capture permission events. | Offer a manual fallback and a clear retry action. | Senior Mobile Architect | Permission failures are understandable and recoverable. |
| Browser incompatibility | Voice or file behavior may differ across mobile browsers. | Medium | High | High | Maintain a supported browser matrix and test critical flows. | Browser-specific error reporting and test matrix. | Disable unsupported flow and show fallback instructions. | Principal Software Architect | Unsupported environments are identified and handled gracefully. |
| IndexedDB corruption | Local persistence may be damaged or partially written. | Low | High | High | Use transactional persistence and integrity checks. | Detect write failures and storage errors. | Recover from backup or draft state. | Principal Software Architect | Storage errors do not silently lose consultation work. |
| Offline sync conflict | Local and server state may conflict during reconnect. | Medium | High | High | Define conflict handling and pending-state rules. | Log sync conflict events. | Preserve the latest confirmed local state and allow review. | DevOps Lead | Sync conflicts are visible and recoverable. |
| Receipt upload failure | Receipt upload and attachment handling may fail. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Keep upload optional and provide retry and fallback paths. | Monitor upload errors. | Allow manual re-upload or skip without blocking consultation completion. | Senior Mobile Architect | Receipt failure does not block the main workflow. |
| Duplicate patient | Search or import may create duplicate patient records. | Medium | High | High | Add duplicate detection and merge rules. | Flag duplicate record events. | Merge or route to review rather than silently duplicating. | Principal Software Architect | Duplicate patient creation is prevented or detected. |
| Timeline corruption | History data may be misordered or inconsistent after refactor. | Medium | High | High | Keep timeline rendering data-driven and validate ordering. | Compare event order and detect anomalies. | Rebuild from persisted events and flag inconsistencies. | Principal Software Architect | Timeline remains ordered and readable after changes. |
| Performance degradation | The app may become sluggish during note capture or save. | Medium | High | High | Use performance budgets and keep live interactions lightweight. | Measure interaction latency and rendering performance. | Defer non-critical work and maintain visible state. | Performance Engineer | Core interactions remain responsive under load. |
| Memory leak | Long sessions may accumulate memory pressure. | Low | Medium | Medium | Avoid stale listeners and repeated state subscriptions. | Observe memory and session duration metrics. | Reload or reset the affected view. | Frontend Architect | Long sessions remain stable without obvious degradation. |
| Battery drain | Continuous voice or background processing may drain the device battery. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Limit background processing and stop unnecessary listeners. | Measure runtime behavior on device. | Disable background processing when not needed. | Senior Mobile Architect | Voice and sync features do not cause excessive battery drain. |
| Large database | The database may grow and slow down queries. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Keep data model lean and archive old records where appropriate. | Monitor database size and query latency. | Archive or compact data when thresholds are crossed. | Principal Software Architect | Core operations remain responsive as data grows. |
| Migration failure | Import or migration work may fail or produce inconsistent data. | Medium | High | High | Use validated import rules and rollback-ready migration steps. | Track migration success and validation failures. | Stop the migration and preserve the existing data set. | QA Director | Import and migration can be validated safely. |
| Rollback failure | A bad release cannot be rolled back cleanly. | Low | High | High | Use feature flags and staged rollout. | Monitor rollout health and error rates. | Disable the affected feature without full rollback. | DevOps Lead | Rollback is possible at the workflow or feature level. |
| AI hallucination | AI-generated content may be inaccurate or overconfident. | Medium | High | Critical | Keep AI assistance reviewable and never treat it as final. | Review AI output and track acceptance/rejection rates. | Remove AI output and revert to manual input. | AI Product Strategist | AI output never bypasses review or confirmation. |
| AI confidence mismatch | The system may present low-confidence suggestions as if they are strong. | Medium | High | Critical | Show confidence explicitly and require review. | Track confidence presentation and acceptance outcomes. | Hide or downgrade low-confidence output. | AI Product Strategist | Confidence is visible and never misleading. |
| Production deployment | Deployment may fail or cause broader availability issues. | Low | High | High | Use phased rollout and staging validation. | Monitor release health and deployment logs. | Roll back the affected release slice quickly. | DevOps Lead | Deployment can be phased and recovered safely. |
| Clinical safety miss | A high-risk action may be completed without sufficient confirmation. | Low | Very High | Critical | Enforce safety gates for prescription and other high-risk flows. | Review critical-action logs and manual QA. | Block the action and require revalidation. | Clinical Workflow Expert | High-risk actions require review and confirmation. |
